Before you call 01

The four things worth having ready.

None of these are required — call and we will work it out. But having them shortens the visit, and sometimes removes the need for a first visit at all.

  • What it does, and when. "Only after heavy rain" or "only when the sprinklers run" narrows a diagnosis faster than anything else.
  • What has already been tried. Including by other people. If inspections found nothing, that is information, not embarrassment.
  • A photo, if you can. The stain, the gap, the fitting, the model plate on the unit.
  • Whether anyone else is involved. Another vendor, a builder still under warranty, an insurer. We have worked alongside all three.
Questions 02

Are you really open 24 hours?

That is the listed hours on our Google profile. Water coming into a house does not wait for business hours, and neither does the phone.

Will you take a job that is genuinely small?

Yes — "small jobs" is the most counted topic across our reviews, mentioned five times. It is the work most people struggle to get anyone to turn up for.

What if other people have already failed to fix it?

Then it is probably interesting. One customer's house had taken on storm water for two years through rounds of inspections before we found the path it was taking.

Can you work with another contractor on site?

Yes, and we have. One documented job required exactly that, plus a reconfiguration the customer described as tricky.

Do you quote before starting?

Yes. "Fair price" and "reasonable price" are the two phrases that recur most across the reviews, and both depend on the number coming first.
